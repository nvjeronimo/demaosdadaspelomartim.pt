    Bottles with the VOLTA symbol?

    Since April 2026, each bottle returned at a Volta point gets you 10 cents back. The machines accept it without the cap.

    You keep your 10 cents and I get the cap!

    More than 50 people have told us they did this with no problems. To be fair, the official rules ask for the bottle with its cap. Always check on the screen that your deposit was counted.

    Hi, I'm Martim. This is my story.

    I was very healthy and happy during the 40 weeks and 6 days I grew in my mum's tummy. Everything changed at birth. I was born in cardiorespiratory arrest and had to be resuscitated.

    A few days later, the doctors explained to my parents that I had suffered hypoxic-ischaemic injury, which damaged my brain. I was diagnosed with Cerebral Palsy – Dystonic Tetraparesis, a condition that challenges every movement I make.

    Keep reading

    Since I was 15 days old I've been followed by physiotherapists at the hospital in Faro. At 4 months I started at APPC Faro, an association for people like me, where I keep making progress every day.

    In recent years, thanks to my parents' tireless efforts, I've been having intensive treatments at Clínica Kinésio, in Espinho: physiotherapy (the Therasuit method), the Padovan method, speech therapy, occupational therapy and transcranial stimulation. Every small victory is a huge joy.

    My life has lots of challenges, but also lots of smiles and achievements. Every gesture of yours is a giant step on my road to independence.

    I have 3 to 4 intensive treatments a year at Clínica Kinésio, in Espinho. Each one lasts a month, away from home with my mum, and costs €6,740.

    Martim

    Frequently asked questions.

    Tap a question to see the answer.

    • What is the Dê uma Tampa project?

      Resialentejo, in Beja, weighs the plastic and metal caps we collect and pays for my treatments directly to the clinic: €411.60 per tonne of plastic and €465.60 per tonne of metal caps. My case number is 167.

    • Where can I drop off the caps?

      There are 14 collection points in Boliqueime, Albufeira, Paderne and Olhos de Água. See the full list under Where to drop off.

    • Why is sorting so important?

      If plastic and metal caps are mixed, the BigBag may be rejected and all the collecting effort is lost.

    • What isn't accepted?
      • Caps from jerrycans and hazardous products
      • Syringe caps and hospital waste
      • Containers of paint, varnish or fats (olive oil, butter, cooking oil)
      • Spray triggers and soap pumps (they have a metal spring)
      • Tupperware lids and toilet cleaner refills
      • Any plastic cap with a metal thread or spring
    • Do cork stoppers count too?

      Only 100% cork ones. Stoppers with plastic or metal tops can't be accepted. Amorim, in Silves, pays €500 per tonne.

    • What about bottles with the VOLTA symbol?

      Return the bottle without its cap, keep your 10 cents and save the cap for me. Keep saving metal caps, shampoo, detergent and milk caps, water-jug handles and cork stoppers too: none of those go into VOLTA.

    • Can I help take the BigBags to Beja?

      Yes, and it's what we struggle with most. If you can transport large loads, talk to us.

    • How do I make a donation?

      Bank transfer to Martim Silva Cruz's donation account: PT50 0045 7214 4036 8604 7382 1. Any amount helps.

    • Can I donate directly to Clínica Kinésio?

      Yes. Call +351 914 032 345 and they'll explain everything. After donating you'll get an invoice with your tax number.

    • Who manages the donations?

      Donations to the account are managed by my parents, only for Martim's treatments and needs, with detailed records. Kinésio always issues an invoice. The Dê uma Tampa money is paid by Resialentejo directly to the clinic.

    • What is cerebral palsy with dystonic tetraparesis?

      It's a neurological injury that affects control of posture and movement. Tetraparesis is the loss of motor function in the arms and legs. Dystonia causes involuntary movements and changes in muscle tone.
