## Platform

web

## Stack

Current site: WordPress 7.x with a purchased Qode theme and Transposh (PT/EN). This round is a proposal (strategy document + mockups), not a rebuild; implementation stack is undecided.

## Users

- **Primary: neighbours in the Algarve (Boliqueime, Albufeira, Paderne, Loulé).** They heard about Martim at school, the health centre, a café or a local event, often via a WhatsApp or Facebook share, and open the site on a phone. Their job: understand quickly what to do with the caps in their kitchen and where to drop them.
- **Secondary: donors anywhere in Portugal** who want to give money (IBAN or directly to Clínica Kinésio) and need to trust where it goes.
- **Tertiary: organisers and partners** (schools, clubs, festivals, businesses, scouts) who want to host a collection point or an event, or help transport BigBags to Beja.

## Product Purpose

Fund Martim Silva Cruz's intensive rehabilitation treatments (Clínica Kinésio, Espinho; 3–4 a year; 2024 budget 6 740 € each). Martim has cerebral palsy (dystonic tetraparesis) after hypoxic-ischemic injury at birth. Success = more caps correctly separated and delivered, more money donated, more community events, and less manual work for his parents.

**Primary action (confirmed): collect bottle caps.** Money donation is second; events and partnerships third.

## Positioning

A family-run, community-powered cause where household waste becomes therapy: plastic caps, metal caps (caricas) and 100% cork stoppers are weighed and converted into treatment funding by named partners, with Martim's own process number (167). Nobody else can claim this mechanism, this village network, or this child's story.

## Operating Context

- "Dê uma Tampa" project, Resialentejo (Beja), process nº 167: 411,60 €/t plastic caps, 465,60 €/t metal caps; Resialentejo pays Kinésio directly.
- Amorim (Silves): 500 €/t of 100% cork stoppers.
- BigBags must be single-material and ≥250 kg (~300–350 kg each). Mixed material can void a whole bag: separation is the critical user behaviour.
- Dad (Mickael) collects jugs from collection points and separates on days off; transport to Beja is the biggest bottleneck.
- 14 official collection points (CD Boliqueime, EB1 Benfarras, Junta de Freguesia de Boliqueime, Centro Comunitário de Vale Silves, EB 2,3 Prof. Dr. Aníbal Cavaco Silva, Lar da Santa Casa de Boliqueime, Centro de Saúde de Boliqueime, Escuteiros 1174, EB1/JI Vale Pedras, EB 2,3 Profª Diamantina Negrão, EB1 Vale Carro, Symbiosis Pub & Bar Olhos de Água, Centro Paroquial de Paderne, Centro de Saúde de Paderne).
- VOLTA deposit system (since April 2026): return bottle without cap, keep 10 cents, keep the cap for Martim (50+ testimonies to July 2026; honest caveat that official rules say bottle with cap).
- Events: walks, padel tournaments, concerts, cake sales, raffles, BFF Solidário.

## Product Principles

1. The cap in your hand is the entry point: make the smallest action obvious first.
2. Show the conversion honestly (kg → € → treatment days) with the real rates.
3. Separation is the make-or-break behaviour: teach it visually, not in paragraphs.
4. Martim's voice and face, never pity: progress, humour, a superhero fist.
5. Reduce the parents' workload: every UX decision should save them a message, a trip or a mixed bag.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Audience includes older residents and phone-first users; large type, high contrast, tap-friendly lists. The subject is disability: respectful language, no inspiration-porn framing.
